## 1. PHASE & STAGE NOMENCLATURE (Read This First)

Three separate frameworks operate in the deck. Don't conflate them.

**Phase 1 / Phase 2** — QuRIE-seq wet-lab data generation phases.
- **Phase 1 (Q3 2026)**: First proprietary batch. 5 donors × 5 timepoints (0/5/30/60/180 min) × 4 modalities directly measured (RNA + Protein + Phospho at all timepoints; ATAC at t=0 and t=180 only). Includes BTK+JAK combination perturbation. Appears on: C1 (canonical), A2, D1, F1.
- **Phase 2 (Q1 2027+)**: Scaled batch. 20 donors. Adds VDJ as 5th modality. Disease-context samples (B-cell lines, CLL, autoimmune) included. Appears on: D1 (canonical), A2, F1.

**Stages 3a / 3b / 3c / 4 / 5** — Model training and architecture phases.
- **Stage 3a (Now → July 2026)**: Public-data engine build. Train encoder + adapter + decomposed readout on public datasets (DOGMA-seq, Mimitou CRISPR, Parse, Rubin). Status: in-flight.
- **Stage 3b (Q4 2026)**: BTK+JAK demo. Zero-shot synergy prediction on QuRIE-seq Phase 1 data. Status: pre-registered methodology, awaits Phase 1 data.
- **Stage 3c (Q1-Q2 2027)**: Causal architecture validation. Neumann propagation + sparse learned GRN + direct-effect log-FC head. Status: spec-locked, validation awaits Phase 1 data.
- **Stage 4 (2027)**: VDJ + 20-donor scale. Validate cross-donor generalization on Phase 2 data.
- **Stage 5 (2028)**: Causal-readiness + clinical handoff. First publishable causal-inference results.

**Layers L1 / L2 / L3 / L4 / L5** — Public-data dataset strategy layers used during Stage 3a engine build.
- **L1**: In-domain CRISPR perturbations — Mimitou ASAP-seq CRISPR sub-study.
- **L2**: Soft perturbation breadth at scale — Parse Cytokine Atlas (9.7M cells × 90 cytokines).
- **L3**: Disease-relevant CRISPR — Rubin Perturb-ATAC (GSE116249, GM12878) as v0; full L3 validation requires internal B-cell CRISPR (Phase 2+).
- **L4**: Temporal time-course — no public dataset adequate; QuRIE-seq Phase 1 functions as L4 directly.
- **L5**: Phosphoproteomics — no public dataset exists for PBMCs under perturbation; closed by QuRIE-seq Phase 1.

## 2. AI / ML TERMINOLOGY

**Adapter strategy** — A small, lightweight neural network layer trained on top of a frozen pretrained encoder. The encoder provides general representations; the adapter learns task-specific behavior. Cheaper than full fine-tuning, and prevents catastrophic forgetting. Used in our architecture per Stage 3 Part 1 ADAPTER_RECOMMENDED verdict.

**AdamW optimizer** — Adam optimizer with decoupled weight decay. Standard for deep learning training in 2024-2026. Used for our encoder pretraining and downstream task training.

**AIVC_GRAD_GUARD** — Environment variable flag (`AIVC_GRAD_GUARD=1`) that blocks gradient flow into the encoder during downstream training. Enforces the frozen-encoder adapter strategy mechanically. Set to `1` in all production training runs after the Stage 3 Part 1 verdict.

**Architecture spec v1.1** — The canonical AIVC GeneLink architecture document. Defines encoder design, decomposed readout, temporal backbone, causal layer. v1.2 with extended causal-layer section is pending (anchored by A5 slide).

**Backbone (encoder backbone)** — The pretrained transformer-encoder weights that produce the 256-dimensional latent representation of multi-omics input. Frozen during downstream training.

**Batch size** — Number of cells processed per gradient update. Larger batches reduce gradient noise but require more GPU memory. Standard ranges: 256-1024 for our encoder training.

**Causal attention mask** — Architectural constraint in the encoder that enforces dependency ordering `ATAC → Phospho → RNA → Protein`. ATAC (chromatin) is the upstream regulatory layer; Protein is the downstream effector. Masking enforces this biological hierarchy in the attention pattern. In-use today in code.

**Centroid-NN (centroid nearest-neighbor)** — Evaluation method. For each held-out test centroid, find the nearest centroid in the training pool by cosine distance; accuracy = fraction where the match is the same label. Used in our 73% cross-corpus Calderon evaluation.

**Chance baseline** — Lower-bound accuracy assuming random guessing. For 5-class cell-type classification (B / T / NK / monocyte / DC), chance = 20%. Our 73% Calderon result is 3.65× chance.

**Compositional generalization** — The model's ability to predict the response of combination perturbations from observation of individual perturbations alone. Example: train on BTK alone + JAK alone, predict BTK+JAK combo response. The 4-arm decomposed readout architecture supports this directly.

**Contrastive multi-omics fusion** — The encoder learns to align RNA, ATAC, and Protein representations of the same cell to be similar (positive pairs) while different cells are dissimilar (negative pairs). Multi-modal contrastive learning. Produces the 256-dimensional latent space.

**Cross-corpus generalization** — The model trained on one dataset (Mimitou DOGMA-seq) generalizes to a different, independently-generated dataset (Calderon 2019) without retraining. Tests for spurious dataset-specific features. Our 73% result demonstrates this property.

**Decomposed readout (4-arm)** — Decoder architecture that decomposes a perturbed cell's predicted state as `h_baseline + Δ_stim + Δ_inhibitor + Δ_synergy`. Each arm models one component independently; synergy is the part not predicted from singles. Enables compositional generalization.

**Direct-effect log-FC head** — Decoder output producing the direct (immediate) perturbation effect vector `dₚ` in log fold-change units. In Stage 3c, this `dₚ` feeds Neumann propagation through the learned GRN to produce the full propagated effect.

**Encoder pretraining** — Self-supervised learning phase where the encoder learns multi-omics representations from large unlabeled datasets (DOGMA-seq, Calderon, Mimitou). Output: 256-D latent space. Foundation for all downstream tasks.

**Foundation model** — Large pretrained model providing general representations for many downstream tasks via fine-tuning or adapter layers. Our encoder is a foundation model for PBMC multi-omics.

**Frozen encoder** — Encoder weights locked during downstream training. Only adapter and decoder heads update. Prevents encoder representations from drifting during task-specific training. Enforced by AIVC_GRAD_GUARD.

**Gradient guard** — See AIVC_GRAD_GUARD.

**Hold-out test set** — Data reserved from training and validation, used only for final evaluation. Prevents test-set leakage and inflated metrics. Calderon 2019 is our cross-corpus hold-out test.

**Indicator function 𝟙[·] (Iverson bracket)** — Mathematical notation. 𝟙[s] = 1 if condition `s` is true, 0 if false. Used in the decomposed readout equation to switch arms on/off based on whether stimulus/inhibitor is present.

**Latent space 256-D** — The 256-dimensional vector representation produced by the encoder. Each cell maps to a point in this 256-D space. Dimensionality chosen to balance expressiveness with computational cost. Smaller than typical transformer hidden states (768+) because multi-omics input is more constrained than natural language.

**Latent SDE (Latent Stochastic Differential Equation)** — Probabilistic temporal model where the latent state evolves with both deterministic drift and stochastic diffusion. Used as fallback if Neural ODE (deterministic) doesn't capture biological noise adequately. Architecture spec v1.1 §7.1.

**L1 regularization** — Penalty on the sum of absolute weight values. Encourages sparsity — many weights pushed to zero. Used in the sparse learned GRN to enforce that most gene-gene edges are zero, retaining only the strongest learned relationships.

**L2 regularization** — Penalty on the sum of squared weight values. Encourages small weights but not exactly zero. Standard regularization for deep learning.

**Learning rate scheduling** — Adjusting the learning rate during training. Typical pattern: warm-up phase (small LR), main phase (large LR), decay phase (small LR). Standard practice.

**Neural ODE (Neural Ordinary Differential Equation)** — Continuous-time dynamics model. Instead of discrete time steps (RNN/transformer), the latent state evolves according to a learned differential equation `dh/dt = f(h, t, θ)`. Handles irregular timepoint spacing (our 0/5/30/60/180 min sampling has unequal gaps) natively. Used in our temporal backbone.

**Neumann propagation `(I − W)⁻¹ dₚ`** — Closed-form solution for perturbation flow through a learned gene regulatory network. `dₚ` is direct effect; `W` is the learned GRN weight matrix; `(I − W)⁻¹` is the matrix inverse propagating the direct effect through all network paths. Requires spectral radius `ρ(W) < 1` for the series to converge. Stage 3c architecture.

**Pre-registered evaluation** — Eval thresholds and methodology committed in writing before running the evaluation. Prevents post-hoc cherry-picking of metrics. Our 73% Calderon result was pre-registered before we ran the evaluation.

**Perturbation embedding** — Vector representation of a perturbation context (which drug/CRISPR/cytokine, at what concentration, for what duration). Combined with cell latent state to predict perturbation response. Stored in the decomposed readout's Δ arms.

**Pseudo-bulk** — Aggregation of single-cell measurements to a coarser unit. For our centroid-NN evaluation, single cells are aggregated by cell-type label within each dataset, producing one centroid vector per cell type per dataset.

**Random projection baseline** — Sanity-check baseline. Replace the encoder with a random linear projection of the input. Measures whether the encoder learns anything beyond what random features capture. Our encoder must exceed this baseline to claim it learned signal.

**Sparse learned GRN (Gene Regulatory Network)** — Matrix `W` where `W_ij` represents the directed regulatory influence of gene `i` on gene `j`. "Sparse" because most entries are zero (enforced by L1 regularization + STRING structural prior). "Learned" because non-zero entries are inferred from perturbation-response data. Stage 3c.

**STIX fontset (matplotlib)** — STIX fonts are a high-quality math typography family. We use STIX in matplotlib mathtext to render the Neumann equation on slide A5 (because cairosvg has glyph substitution bugs for italic Latin letters in plain SVG text).

**TF-IDF baseline (Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency)** — Bag-of-words text-style baseline for evaluating whether the encoder learns more than gene-frequency patterns. Each gene treated as a token; cells represented by their TF-IDF vector over genes. Our encoder must beat this to claim it captures cell biology beyond frequency statistics.

**Vector representation (embedding)** — Generic term for representing complex objects (cells, perturbations, genes) as numerical vectors in a high-dimensional space. Distances in the space correspond to similarity. Standard ML practice.

## 3. BIOLOGY & MULTI-OMICS TERMINOLOGY

**Adapter (in biology context)** — Conflict-avoidance note: "adapter" in the AI/ML section means a neural network layer (above). In wet-lab biology, "adapter" can mean a DNA sequence for library preparation. Context disambiguates.

**ASAP-seq** — "ATAC + Surface protein Antibody Profiling sequencing". Single-cell method measuring ATAC (chromatin accessibility) + surface proteins simultaneously. Variant of CITE-seq. Mimitou 2021's CRISPR sub-study used ASAP-seq with hashtag-oligo-encoded CRISPR.

**ATAC-seq (Assay for Transposase-Accessible Chromatin)** — Method measuring chromatin accessibility — which parts of the genome are open for transcription factor binding. Single-cell variant is scATAC-seq. Slow-varying timescale relative to RNA expression (justifies sampling at t=0 and t=180 only in Phase 1).

**BCR (B-cell receptor)** — Antigen receptor on B cells. Signaling pathway includes BTK as a key kinase. Combination targeting BCR (BTK) + JAK pathways is the BTK+JAK combo demo basis.

**BCR pathway** — Signal transduction cascade from B-cell receptor activation through BTK and downstream effectors (NFKB, etc.). Disrupted in CLL; Ibrutinib (BTK inhibitor) is approved CLL therapy.

**BTK (Bruton tyrosine kinase)** — Kinase central to BCR signaling. Therapeutic target in CLL. Ibrutinib is the FDA-approved BTK inhibitor. The "B" in BTK+JAK demo.

**Calderon 2019** — Published dataset of immune cells under stimulation, used as our cross-corpus hold-out test for the encoder. We achieve 73% pseudo-bulk centroid-NN accuracy on Calderon despite training on Mimitou — demonstrates cross-corpus generalization.

**CD3E** — T-cell receptor complex component (CD3-epsilon chain). Marker for T cells. Used as a perturbation target in Mimitou CRISPR sub-study and in our Stage 3 Part 1 probe.

**CD4** — Co-receptor on helper T cells. Cell-type marker; also used as perturbation target in our Stage 3 Part 1 work.

**Chromatin accessibility** — Whether a region of DNA is open for transcription factor binding. Measured by ATAC-seq. Slow-varying on minute timescales (justifies sparse temporal sampling in our Phase 1 design).

**CITE-seq (Cellular Indexing of Transcriptomes and Epitopes by sequencing)** — Single-cell method measuring RNA + surface proteins via antibody-derived tags. Core method used in DOGMA-seq and other multi-omics protocols.

**CLL (Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia)** — B-cell cancer. BTK is a clinical target via Ibrutinib. JAK pathway also implicated. Our BTK+JAK demo connects to CLL clinical literature (PMID 26819050, NCT02912754).

**Compositional perturbation** — A perturbation made of multiple components (e.g., BTK + JAK combination). The opposite is a "single perturbation" (BTK alone or JAK alone). Compositional generalization predicts compositional perturbations from singles.

**CRISPR (Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats)** — Genome editing tool. In our context, CRISPR-based perturbation = knocking out specific genes to observe consequences on cell state.

**Cytokine** — Signaling molecule released by cells. Bind to receptors and trigger pathways (e.g., JAK-STAT pathway). Parse Cytokine Atlas uses 90 cytokines for perturbation breadth.

**DOGMA-seq** — Triple-modality single-cell method measuring RNA + ATAC + surface protein on the same cell. From Mimitou 2021 (Nature Biotechnology). The training dataset for our encoder pretraining.

**Donor metadata** — Demographic and clinical information about blood donors. Sanquin (Dutch blood bank) provides blood-type-only for privacy. Our encoder uses ATAC at t=0 as a chromatin-grounded donor signature in absence of demographic metadata.

**Effector gene** — Gene encoding a protein with direct biological action (e.g., cytokine production). Downstream of transcription factors in the regulatory hierarchy.

**Foundation cell types / lineages** — Major immune cell populations in PBMCs: B cells, T cells (CD4 + CD8 subsets), NK cells, monocytes, dendritic cells (DC). Our encoder evaluated on 5-class lineage classification.

**Gene Ontology (GO)** — Standardized vocabulary for gene functions (biological processes, molecular functions, cellular components). Used in our Stage 3 Part 1 Report 3 for pathway enrichment analysis on encoder-learned representations.

**HTO (HashTag Oligonucleotide)** — Short DNA barcode used to multiplex samples in single-cell experiments. In Mimitou ASAP-seq CRISPR, HTOs encode which CRISPR guide perturbed each cell. Read out alongside the cellular ATAC/RNA/protein data.

**IMGT (international ImMunoGeneTics)** — Database of immune-receptor (TCR/BCR) gene segments. Reference for VDJ analysis (Phase 2 relevance).

**Inhibitor (drug)** — Small molecule that blocks a specific protein function. BTK inhibitors block BCR signaling; JAK inhibitors block cytokine signaling. Used in Phase 1 perturbation panel.

**Iono (Ionomycin)** — Calcium ionophore commonly paired with PMA for non-specific cell stimulation. PMA/Iono is a standard QC stimulus combination in immune cell experiments.

**JAK (Janus kinase)** — Family of kinases central to cytokine signaling. JAK-STAT pathway downstream effector. Ruxolitinib is the FDA-approved JAK inhibitor. The "J" in BTK+JAK demo.

**JAK-STAT signaling** — Pathway from cytokine receptor → JAK kinases → STAT transcription factors → gene expression. Key immune signaling axis. Therapeutic target in multiple cancers.

**Kinase** — Enzyme that phosphorylates proteins (adds phosphate group). Activation/deactivation switch in signaling pathways. BTK, JAK, ZAP70 are all kinases. Phospho-proteomics measures kinase activity directly.

**Lineage** — Cell-type category (B, T, NK, monocyte, DC). Classification used in our 5-class evaluation.

**Marker (cell-type marker)** — Protein or transcript characteristically expressed by a specific cell type (e.g., CD3 for T cells, CD19 for B cells). Used to identify cell populations.

**Mimitou 2021** — Mimitou et al., Nature Biotechnology 2021 paper introducing DOGMA-seq + ASAP-seq methods. Source of our encoder pretraining data (DOGMA-seq sub-study) and Stage 3 Part 1 perturbation probe (ASAP-seq CRISPR sub-study).

**MoA (Mechanism of Action)** — How a drug produces its therapeutic effect at the molecular level. Phospho-proteomics directly reveals MoA by showing which kinases activate/deactivate.

**Multi-omics** — Simultaneous measurement of multiple molecular data types (omics: RNA = transcriptome, ATAC = chromatin, protein = proteome, phospho = phospho-proteome). Our platform's defining capability.

**MYD88** — Innate immunity signaling adapter. Cross-pathway hub between TLR signaling and downstream effectors. Used as illustrative node in A5 GRN visualization.

**NFKB (Nuclear Factor kappa B)** — Transcription factor central to immune signaling. Hub node downstream of BCR and TLR pathways. Used as illustrative node in A5 GRN visualization.

**NK cell (Natural Killer)** — Innate lymphocyte cell type. Part of PBMC lineage set.

**Parse Cytokine Atlas (Parse Bio)** — Large public dataset: 9.7M PBMC cells × 90 cytokine perturbations. Our L2 (soft perturbation breadth) public training layer. RNA-only.

**PBMC (Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cell)** — Blood cells with single round nucleus: lymphocytes (B, T, NK) + monocytes + dendritic cells. Source population for our wet-lab work (Sanquin blood). Not cancer cell lines.

**Perturbation** — Anything that changes cell state: drug treatment, CRISPR knockout, cytokine stimulation. Our platform predicts perturbation responses.

**Perturb-ATAC** — Combined CRISPR perturbation + ATAC-seq readout. Rubin et al. published GSE116249 (GM12878 B-cell line) — our L3-v0 dataset.

**Phospho-proteomics (phospho)** — Measurement of phosphorylated proteins. Reveals kinase activation state (mechanism, not consequence). Integral to QuRIE-seq assay; available to us in Phase 1 (Q3 2026). No public single-cell phospho dataset exists for PBMCs under perturbation.

**Phospho-signaling** — Signal transduction via protein phosphorylation. Kinase pathways (BTK, JAK, ZAP70) operate through phospho-signaling. Phospho-proteomics is the direct readout.

**Phospho timing** — Phospho responses occur in minutes after perturbation (faster than transcriptional changes). The 5-minute timepoint in QuRIE-seq Phase 1 captures phospho signal that no other multi-omics dataset measures at single-cell resolution.

**PMA (Phorbol Myristate Acetate)** — Phorbol ester that activates PKC and downstream signaling. PMA/Iono = standard non-specific stimulation cocktail.

**Primary cell (primary biology)** — Cell freshly isolated from an organism (e.g., human blood). Distinguished from cell lines (immortalized in vitro). Primary cells are more biologically representative but harder to work with. Our PBMCs are primary.

**Protein (in our context)** — Surface marker proteins measured by CITE-seq via antibody-derived tags. 30-210 markers depending on panel. Distinguished from intracellular phospho-proteins (measured by QuRIE-seq).

**Reactome** — Pathway database. Reference for cell-state transition pathways and downstream interpretation. Used in our partial pathway annotation work.

**Rubin Perturb-ATAC (GSE116249)** — Published dataset: TF perturbations (PAX5, EBF1, IRF4, EZH2) in GM12878 EBV-transformed B-cell line, ATAC readout. Our L3-v0 layer.

**Sanquin** — Dutch national blood bank. Source of blood for our Phase 1 wet-lab. Returns blood-type-only metadata per privacy policy.

**scATAC-seq / scRNA-seq** — Single-cell variants of ATAC and RNA sequencing. "sc" = single-cell. Each cell sequenced individually rather than bulk averaging.

**Sci [PENDING IDENTIFICATION]** — Reference to a "Sci"-prefix term Kinga mentioned in her speaker notes ask. Systematic scan of slide content and content specs found no "Sci"-prefix library, methodology, or term. May be one of: SciPlex (Srivatsan 2020 single-cell perturbation atlas), sci-RNA-seq (Cao et al. method family), Sci-Hub reference, or potentially a misread. Awaiting Kinga clarification on review.

**Soskic 2025** — CD4 T-cell CRISPRi dataset considered for L3 layer; not adopted because limited to T cells only (not full PBMC scope) and weak disease-context relevance.

**STAT3 (Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription 3)** — Transcription factor downstream of JAK signaling. Hub in JAK-STAT pathway. Used as illustrative node in A5 GRN visualization.

**STRING database (STRING v12.0)** — Protein-Protein Interaction database. Provides edge-existence priors for sparse learned GRN (Stage 3c). STRING-supported edges face lower L1 sparsity pressure; novel edges remain learnable.

**Stimulation / stim** — Treatment that activates immune cells (e.g., PMA/Iono, cytokine treatment). Used as the "stim" arm in the 4-arm decomposed readout.

**Surface protein (surface marker)** — Protein on the cell membrane, accessible to antibody staining without cell permeabilization. Measured by CITE-seq. 30-210 markers in our typical panels.

**Synergy** — When the combined effect of two perturbations exceeds the sum of their individual effects. Mathematically: `Δ_combo > Δ_drug1 + Δ_drug2`. The "Δ_synergy" arm in the decomposed readout captures this directly.

**TCR (T-cell receptor)** — Antigen receptor on T cells. Signaling pathway includes ZAP70 (TCR signaling kinase) and CD3 complex. Studied in our public-data work.

**TCR signaling** — Pathway from T-cell receptor → ZAP70 → downstream effectors. Disrupted in autoimmune disease and some cancers.

**Timepoint (TP)** — Specific time after perturbation when measurement is made. Our Phase 1 design: 0/5/30/60/180 minutes.

**Transcription factor (TF)** — Protein that regulates gene transcription. Hub node in gene regulatory networks. Examples: NFKB, STAT3, IRF7. Hub nodes in our A5 GRN visualization.

**VDJ (Variable, Diversity, Joining)** — Gene rearrangement process generating immune receptor diversity (TCR and BCR). VDJ-seq measures these rearrangements. Not in Phase 1; added in Phase 2 (2027) as 5th modality.

**Vehicle control** — Untreated baseline. Used as reference for measuring perturbation effects. The "h_baseline" arm in the decomposed readout.

**ZAP70 (Zeta-chain-associated protein kinase 70)** — Kinase central to T-cell receptor signaling. Studied in our Stage 3 Part 1 work and in BTK+JAK comparison context.

## 7. MATHEMATICAL NOTATION KEY

**`𝟙[s]` or `1[s]` (Iverson bracket)** — Indicator function. Reads as "1 if condition s is true, 0 if false". Used in decomposed readout to switch arms on/off.

**`Δ` (Delta)** — "Change" or "difference". Used in decomposed readout for the individual perturbation effects (Δ_stim, Δ_inh, Δ_synergy).

**`d_p` or `dₚ`** — Direct perturbation effect vector. Subscript `p` means "perturbation". Read as "d-sub-p" or "direct effect of perturbation p".

**`h` (h_base, h_baseline)** — Hidden state vector for a cell. "h" comes from "hidden" in neural network literature. h_base = vehicle-control baseline state.

**`(I − W)⁻¹`** — Matrix inverse expression. `I` is identity matrix. `W` is learned GRN matrix. `(I − W)⁻¹` is the Neumann series sum that propagates direct effects through the network.

**`ρ(W) < 1` (spectral radius condition)** — Architectural requirement for Neumann propagation. `ρ(W)` is the spectral radius (largest absolute eigenvalue) of W. Must be less than 1 for the Neumann series to converge. Enforced by L1 sparsity.

**`ŷ` (y-hat)** — Predicted output (in our case, predicted gene expression or response). Hat distinguishes from observed `y`.

**Subscripts `_p`, `_stim`, `_inh`, `_synergy`** — Tags identifying which component of the decomposed readout. p = perturbation generic; stim = stimulus arm; inh = inhibitor arm; synergy = synergy arm.
